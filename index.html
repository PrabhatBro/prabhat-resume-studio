Frequently asked questions

A few details before you place an order.

Will I receive both PDF and Word files?

Yes. Each listed package includes a PDF copy and an editable Word (.docx) copy.

Can you make a resume if I have no work experience?

Yes. A fresher resume can focus on education, projects, coursework, skills, volunteering and relevant activities that you actually completed.

Do you guarantee an interview or job?

No. A resume can help present your background clearly, but interviews and hiring decisions are made by employers.

How does the order form work?
